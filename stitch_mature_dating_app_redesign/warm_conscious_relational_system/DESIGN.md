## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intersection of vibrant romantic optimism and grounded relational maturity. It unites the energetic, high-conviction presence of modern dating leaders with the thoughtful, editorial clarity required for emotional awareness, boundary architecture, and conscious partnership.

The visual style is **Contemporary Warm Minimalist with Editorial Nuance**. It relies on crisp surfaces, generous whitespace, confident typography, and purposeful interactive micro-surfaces. The atmosphere is intentional rather than gamified:
- **Tone:** Empathetic, assertive, discerning, and radiant.
- **Audience:** Emotionally mature daters, intentional relationship builders, and self-aware communicators seeking depth over dopamine loops.
- **Visual Contrast:** High-clarity charcoal-on-ivory text foundations electrified with warm golden amber and sunset coral highlights, creating an approachable yet refined cadence.

## Colors

The palette balances buoyant attraction with grounded emotional safety. 

- **Primary (`#FFC629`)**: Inspired by Bumble's signature golden amber, conveying vitality, optimism, and warmth. Used for primary badges, prominent floating indicators, and highlight markers.
- **Secondary (`#FF5A5F`)**: A vivid sunset coral, bringing passionate romance, forward momentum, and focused conversion to CTA buttons and critical engagement touchpoints.
- **Tertiary (`#1F4E46`)**: An intentional forest pine green that anchors the interface with psychological safety, relational groundedness, and stability.
- **Neutral (`#191919`)**: A deep, velvety charcoal for typography and solid states, providing high-contrast editorial hierarchy without the sterile severity of pure black.

Complementing named neutrals and functional semantic tints:
- **`surface-cream` (`#FAF8F5`)** and **`surface-subtle` (`#F3EFEA`)**: Offer organic warmth behind interactive cards and interactive prompt bays.
- **`growth-sage` & `growth-sage-soft`**: Define self-regulation metrics, alignment cues, and secure-state indicators.
- **`dilemma-rose`**: Hosts critical reflexions, boundaries, and friction-pattern callouts with high emotional clarity.

## Typography

The type system is powered by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, providing clean geometric curves, confident apertures, and humanist warmth. It mirrors the bold, friendly character of top-tier modern social platforms while preserving pristine legibility in deep editorial contexts.

- **Display Scales (`display-xl`, `display-lg`)**: Bold, structural weights designed to announce brand thesis statements with impact.
- **Editorial Sub-Scales**: Pair standard geometric weights with occasional italicized styling in hero sub-clauses (e.g., _Maturity Makes It Simple_) to introduce literary intimacy.
- **Body & Captions**: Generous line-heights are enforced across medium and large body text (`1.6x` factor) to give reflective dilemmas, communication scripts, and prompts room to breathe.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an intentional **12-column responsive fluid grid** with strict max-width constraints on interactive card stacks to preserve intimacy:

- **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: 12 columns, 32px gutters, 48px outer margins. Relational dilemma modules, telemetry panes, and dual-state comparisons split into clean 2-column or 3-column comparative cards. Max container width: `1240px`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, 24px gutters, 32px margins. Side-by-side metric dashboards stack beneath central interactive card viewports.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 20px gutters, 16px margins. Cards span the full safe width with swipe gestures and fixed bottom pill action anchors.

Rhythm adheres to a predictable 4px/8px modular base, ensuring visual alignment across nested metadata pills, dilemma headers, and prompt buttons.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages **warm diffused ambient shadows** paired with **subtle border definition** rather than harsh drop shadows. This generates a tactile, inviting card aesthetic that feels liftable, approachable, and calm:

- **Flat/Base Surface**: Pure `#FFFFFF` resting on `#FAF8F5`, bounded by a 1px border of `#E7E2DA`.
- **Card Rest (`elevation-sm`)**: Used for stationary list items, badges, and inactive scenarios.
  - `box-shadow: 0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.04), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.03);`
- **Floating Interactive (`elevation-md`)**: Used for the central swipeable scenario cards, profile stacks, and active decision bays.
  - `box-shadow: 0px 16px 36px -6px rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.07), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.04);`
- **Overlay/Modal (`elevation-lg`)**: Pinned bottom drawer trays and interactive dilemma inspection sheets.
  - `box-shadow: 0px 24px 48px -8px rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.12), 0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.04);`

## Shapes

The design system adheres to a **Pill-shaped (Level 3)** geometry, prioritizing organic softness, touch ergonomics, and warmth. 

- **Action Buttons & Chips**: Fully pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`) to invite touch and echo the fluid interaction patterns of top-tier consumer apps.
- **Interactive Dilemma & Scenario Cards**: `rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl` (24px to 32px), creating a smooth, friendly enclosure that protects against harsh edges.
- **Form Controls & Nested Selectors**: Soft rounded-xl corners (16px) maintaining harmony with larger containing cards.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action (Sunset Coral)**: Solid `#FF5A5F` background, white label, full pill radius (`9999px`), 14px vertical and 28px horizontal padding. Subtle scale down to `0.97` on press with smooth easing.
- **Secondary Action (Warm Minimal)**: Solid `#191919` or transparent with 1.5px `#E7E2DA` border, high-contrast dark text, pill-shaped.
- **Swipe Action Nodes**: Circular 56px and 64px floating action triggers (e.g., Regulate / Growth Space) with tinted inner icons and ambient drop shadows.

### Chips & Badges
- **Context Pill**: Ultra-compact (height 26px), soft amber (`#FFF8E7`), coral, or sage background with uppercase label typography (`label-sm`). Includes an optional 6px pulsing relational indicator dot.
- **Filter & Selection Chips**: 36px height, rounded full pill, bordered by `#E7E2DA`. Active state transitions to `#191919` fill with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Interactive Dilemma & Profile Cards
- **Dilemma Card Stack**: Contained within an elevated, multi-layered shell (32px border radius). Top bar features an EQ score badge and situational tag. Center block features scenario narrative in crisp `headline-sm` with sub-context in `body-sm`. Bottom block nests dual high-context communication scripts.
- **Flippable Scenario Surface**: Supports horizontal drag gestures or instant micro-tap toggles to reveal reactive vs. mature response loops.

### Lists & Form Fields
- **Prompt Input Fields**: 52px height, soft pill/rounded container (16px), `#FAF8F5` background, 1px border in `#E7E2DA`. On focus: 1.5px border highlight in `#FFC629` with zero harsh outline rings.
- **Radio & Scenario Selectors**: Interactive response tiles framed by 1px borders; clicking elevates the container, paints the border `#191919`, and displays a checkmark badge.
